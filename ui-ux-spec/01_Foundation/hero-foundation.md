# Greentomato Home Hero Foundation

## Direction

Mineral Green Editorial Future: a cinematic, low-saturation opening built from
negative space, a restrained central statement, a cropped official wordmark,
and an atmospheric mineral light field.

## Tokens

- Background: `#090b09`
- Foreground: `#e7e6dd`
- Official brand green: `#69a84f`
- Mineral shadow: `#151a14`
- Header inset: `clamp(18px, 2vw, 34px)`
- Primary easing: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`

## Composition

- Fixed-height opening: `100svh`, minimum `680px`
- Header: left logo, centered navigation, right language/project actions
- Narrative copy: centered around 31–46% of the viewport height; narrow measure
- Wordmark: bottom-aligned and intentionally cropped by the viewport
- Background: diffuse radial light with grain; no literal copy of reference assets

## Responsive rule

On compact screens the navigation collapses, the central copy moves upward,
and the official wordmark stays full-width with less aggressive cropping.
